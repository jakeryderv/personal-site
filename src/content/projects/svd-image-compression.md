Decomposes grayscale images into U, Σ, Vᵀ and reconstructs low-rank approximations at a
chosen k, with functions for energy-retention and compression-ratio metrics. Includes
visualization tools (singular value plots, energy curves, side-by-side reconstructions
with error maps) and a tutorial notebook walking through the math.
